    \section{UPEC}
    \textit{09/2009 - 06/2013}

    Université Paris-Est Créteil (UPEC) is a public multidisciplinary university located in the Paris region, offering undergraduate and graduate programs across science, engineering, health, and social sciences.

    It serves a large and diverse student population and is part of the French public higher education system.

    The IUT Sénart-Fontainebleau, a technical faculty of UPEC, provides vocational and professionally oriented programs in applied sciences and information technology.

    The Fontainebleau campus of IUT Sénart-Fontainebleau focuses on smaller cohorts and close collaboration with local industries, delivering practice-oriented training through programs such as the University Diploma of Technology (DUT) and professional bachelor's degrees.
